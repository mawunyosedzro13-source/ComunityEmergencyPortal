Safety Tips

Simple everyday precautions that help prevent emergencies before they happen.

🏠 Home Safety

  • Keep a working fire extinguisher and smoke detector at home.
  • Store matches, lighters, and chemicals out of children's reach.
  • Know where your main gas, water, and electricity shut-offs are.
  • Keep emergency numbers posted somewhere visible.

🚗 Road Safety

  • Always wear a seatbelt and ensure children use car seats.
  • Avoid using your phone while driving.
  • Obey speed limits, especially near schools and residential areas.
  • Keep a basic emergency kit in your car (torch, first aid kit, water).

🌊 Natural Disaster Preparedness

  • Know your community's evacuation routes and shelters.
  • Keep an emergency bag ready with essentials (water, food, torch, documents).
  • Stay updated with local weather and disaster alerts.
  • Have a family communication plan in case you get separated.

🔒 Personal Safety

  • Stay aware of your surroundings, especially at night.
  • Share your location with a trusted person when traveling alone.
  • Keep your phone charged and emergency contacts saved.
  • Trust your instincts — leave a situation that feels unsafe.

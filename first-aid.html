First Aid Tips

Basic first aid steps to help while you wait for professional medical help. These are general guidelines — always call an ambulance for serious injuries.

🩸 Cuts & Bleeding

  1. Wash your hands before touching the wound if possible.
  2. Apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth or bandage.
  3. Raise the injured area above heart level if you can.
  4. Keep pressure on until bleeding slows, then bandage the wound.
  5. Seek medical help if bleeding doesn't stop or the cut is deep.

🔥 Burns

  1. Cool the burn under cool (not icy) running water for about 10–20 minutes.
  2. Remove tight clothing or jewellery near the burn before it swells.
  3. Cover loosely with a clean, non-fluffy cloth.
  4. Do not apply ice, butter, or ointments to the burn.
  5. Seek medical attention for large, deep, or facial burns.

💨 Choking

  1. Ask the person if they can cough or speak — encourage coughing if they can.
  2. If they cannot breathe, cough, or speak, give up to 5 sharp back blows between the shoulder blades.
  3. If that doesn't work, give up to 5 abdominal thrusts.
  4. Call emergency services immediately if the person becomes unresponsive.
⚠️ These steps are general awareness information, not a substitute for certified first aid training. Consider taking a hands-on first aid or CPR course.

🤕 Fainting

  1. Lay the person down and raise their legs slightly.
  2. Loosen tight clothing around the neck.
  3. Ensure fresh air and give them space.
  4. If they don't recover within a minute, call for medical help.

🚑 When to Call an Ambulance

  • Difficulty breathing or no breathing
  • Severe or uncontrolled bleeding
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Signs of a heart attack or stroke
  • Suspected broken bones or head injury
